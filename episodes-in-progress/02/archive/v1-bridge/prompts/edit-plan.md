# Episode 06 — Edit Plan

## Structure
Two-shot, total ~24s usable from two 15s Seedance generations. Hard cut between Part A and Part B at the kiss-frame.

## Part A (Seedance, 15s)
Reference image: `reference/ref-a.png` — sloth alone on jungle-bridge branch, no people. Generated by Nano Banana 2 from `prompts/scene-prompt-a.txt`.

Beats:
- 0–2s HOOK — close-up sloth, Jack off-cam whisper "babe — babe babe look —"
- 2–7s SETUP — Camila on bridge crouched, hand over mouth, "no no no, no he's like — he's right —" / "I KNOW —" / "shh shh shh shh —"
- 7–12s PUCKER — Camila slowly leans, hesitates, "wait. wait wait wait. okay. is he gonna, like — um — is he gonna bite or —" / "I don't… I don't think they have teeth —" / "you DON'T think?" / "babe just — just —"
- 12–14s ACCIDENTAL KISS — bridge sways, Camila pitches forward, lips on sloth's nose. "shi—" cut off / sharp inhale.
- 14–15s HOLD — frozen on lips-against-nose tableau, sloth eyes wide. **This is the cut frame.**

## Part B (Seedance, 15s)
Reference image: `reference/ref-b.png` — described kiss tableau (Camila's lips pressed to sloth nose, both frozen). Generated by Nano Banana 2 from `prompts/scene-prompt-b.txt`. Deliberately departs from the no-people-in-reference rule because Part B is a continuation shot, not an opening shot.

Beats:
- 0–3s HOLD — same tableau, sloth blinks slowly, "...oh my god." / "don't move. don't — like — don't —"
- 3–9s SLOW TILT — sloth slowly tilts head toward Camila over 6s, "what is he — why is he —" / "he's — he's ACTUALLY —" / "oh my GOD —"
- 9–12s KISS-BACK LANDS — sloth presses lips into hers, "he's kissing you back." / "I can't, I can't, I can't —"
- 12–15s TAIL — kiss held, "oh my god." reverent whisper.

## Stitch
Hard cut, no cutaway. Part A's last frame and Part B's first frame should both depict the same tableau (Camila's lips pressed to sloth's nose, both frozen, sloth's eyes open and wide). The cut works as long as Seedance regenerates Camila with reasonably similar likeness across the two halves — small differences will read as "different angle / camera reset" in UGC pretense.

If the cut reads poorly on review, fallback options:
1. **Trim Part A**: Cut Part A earlier on the bridge sway — before lips touch — and let Part B begin with the lips landing. Removes the seam but loses the freeze-on-contact moment.
2. **Cutaway insert**: Add a 0.5–1s reaction-style B-roll between the two halves (e.g., extreme close-up of the sloth's eye blinking, or a tilt-down to the rope bridge planks). Cleanly hides the seam at the cost of breaking the locked-handheld viewpoint.
3. **Literal frame stitch (advanced)**: Extract Part A's last frame, host it on a public URL, pass that URL directly to Seedance Part B as `image_url`. Skips Nano Banana 2 for Part B entirely. Untested.

## FFmpeg stitch command
```
ffmpeg -y -i raw/raw-a.mp4 -i raw/raw-b.mp4 \
  -filter_complex "[0:v][0:a][1:v][1:a]concat=n=2:v=1:a=1[v][a]" \
  -map "[v]" -map "[a]" \
  -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -c:a aac -b:a 192k \
  raw/raw-stitched.mp4
```
