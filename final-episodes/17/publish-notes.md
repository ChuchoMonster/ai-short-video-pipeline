- **Production date**: 2026-05-18 / 2026-05-19
- **Format breakthroughs validated this episode**:
  - **Anti-centering as a top-line rule.** "If a shot looks balanced/symmetric/composed, it's wrong — knock it off-center." Both animal AND people pushed hard off-center, on diagonals, half-cropped by the frame edge. The kisser never squared-up directly hovering above the animal. Saved to memory at `feedback_kwa_anti_centering.md`.
  - **Cut INTO the kiss.** Instead of rendering the awkward "subject hovering above animal" hover beat, the SHARP CUT lands on lips-already-touching — preserves the realism of "phone-edited UGC" and avoids the staged-hover tell.
  - **Loose-far reveal distance.** Reveal beat stays back at handheld-bystander distance, never pushes in to a centered close-up — the group mills/celebrates loosely, the kisser is decentred even at the payoff hold. Anti-cinematic.
  - **Delayed-reveal structure.** The educational payload is NOT a cold open — it lands as a gut-punch AT THE END (Dylan SHOUTS the fact reading off his phone), and the kisser reacts AFTER the fact. Inverts the Ep16 cold-open-narration structure.
  - **Night-phone authenticity multiplier.** Heavy low-light noise, blown pool-light hot spot, mixed blue/orange light, motion-blur softness — the noisy phone-at-night look is a strong realism layer.
  - **Reused cached Nano Banana ref across iterations.** Held setting/animal constant via `seedance-from-url.sh` while only iterating the Seedance prompt. Saved $0.36 on regens and removed setting-drift risk during prompt iteration.
