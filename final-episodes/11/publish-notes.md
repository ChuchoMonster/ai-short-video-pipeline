- **Animal**: real wild hellbender salamander (Cryptobranchus alleganiensis, ~22 inches, small-house-cat-sized but flat and stretched-out; mottled muted brown-grey-tan with darker irregular blotches, slight greenish-algae cast, wrinkly-skin folds along the sides, wide flat bulldog-shaped head, tiny black eyes nearly hidden, wet slimy glistening skin)
- **Setting**: shallow rocky Appalachian creek bed in West Virginia mid-morning, dappled sun through hemlock canopy, mossy stones, clear flowing water, hemlock-needle litter; muted brown-grey-green palette
- **Cast**: Uncle Steve (~45, on-screen, white American Appalachian outdoorsman, weathered tan face with stubble + faded brown ball cap pulled low + faded olive long-sleeve flannel + dark olive-brown chest waders; slightly heavyset, real wrinkles and sun-damage) + Cody (off-camera operator, his ~17-year-old nephew, casual Appalachian American voice, sloppy one-handed filming)
- **Payoff structure (type #7, hidden anatomy reveal)**: hook on hellbender alone with mouth sealed → Steve wades in, hesitates at distance, commits → physical lip-peck on wet flat head (mouth still sealed during contact) → the MOMENT Steve's lips break contact, hellbender slowly opens its wide flat mouth in a defensive yawn revealing the wide wet PINK-GUMMY interior over ~1 second → both Steve and Cody react in REAL contained laugh-disbelief (not theatrical, not screaming)
- **Generation source**: `episodes-in-progress/11/raw/raw.mp4` (v2 — second iteration). v1 archived (acting was projected/performative — "talking to the audience not each other," scripted-sounding clean phrases like "well I'll be"). v2 = ACTING REALISM RULE applied (private low murmured voices, mumbled fragments, aggressive dialogue trim, no clean scripted phrases).
- **Cost to produce**: ~$3.88 (one image $0.12 + two Seedance attempts $1.88 each, image reused on v2)
- **Production date**: 2026-05-10
- **Format breakthroughs**:
  - **ACTING REALISM RULE** introduced and validated. Counters Seedance's default "TikTok-creator voice" with explicit prompt language: "characters unaware they're being filmed for an audience," "voices at private low murmured volume not projected," "mumbled imperfect speech with half-finished thoughts," "no clean scripted-sounding phrases," "real contained laughter not theatrical AHAHA." Captured in `feedback_kwa_acting_realism.md` — now a permanent rule for all future episodes.
  - **Aggressive dialogue trimming.** Total spoken utterances in this 15s video: ~7 short mumbled fragments. Most beats are just breath + ambient creek water. Validates the "if in doubt reduce dialogue" heuristic.
  - **Reveal-after-kiss structure.** First episode where the payoff animal-behavior happens AFTER lip-contact ends rather than during. Pacing-wise this works because the kiss is committed (Steve has already done it) before the reveal, so the reaction is "what did I just kiss?" rather than "I'm seeing this mid-kiss."
  - **Sloppy intentionally-unpolished camera work.** Validates that explicit "the whole video should feel like Cody is barely keeping the camera pointed in the right direction" prompt language reads as authentic UGC rather than failed cinematography.
